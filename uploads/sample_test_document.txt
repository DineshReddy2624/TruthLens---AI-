Global Milestones in Space Exploration and Clean Energy

In recent decades, international investments in solar photovoltaics have surged exponentially. According to the International Energy Agency, solar power has become one of the cheapest sources of new electricity generation in history.

Meanwhile, NASA's James Webb Space Telescope operates at the second Sun-Earth Lagrange point roughly 1.5 million kilometers from Earth, transmitting deep infrared observations of early cosmic structures.

However, fringe internet blogs have claimed that the James Webb telescope discovered an alien metallic sphere orbiting Proxima Centauri, although international astronomical observatories have confirmed that no such artificial artifact has ever been detected.
